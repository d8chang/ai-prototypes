Reset privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Reset is a small, free wellness experiment by Deborah Chang. It is built to keep as little about you as possible.

Your phone vs. what Deborah sees

On your phoneDeborah sees
Your nameYesNo
Journal, vent, gratitudeYesNo
The exact idea, like "walk around the block"YesNo, only the type, like "movement"
Mood, time, placeYesYes, as anonymous taps
Did it, did it helpYesYes, as anonymous taps
Who you areYesNo, just a random id

Deborah sees rows like "Random id 7f3k, Tuesday: low mood, 5 minutes, at a desk, a movement idea, did it, it helped." She can tell the same anonymous person came back, but not who they are. This shows whether Reset helps people. Delete my data removes your rows.

Reminders (if you turn them on)

To send notifications, Reset keeps a random device id, the notification address your phone gives it, your reminder time, how often, and your time zone. No name or email. Turning reminders off or deleting your data removes it.

What Reset never does

  • Sell or share your data.
  • Use ads or trackers.
  • Ask for your email, contacts, location or health records.

Where it's stored

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Reminder data and anonymous counts are kept in a Google Sheet that only Deborah can access, and reminders are sent through your phone's own notification service (Apple or Google).

Delete your data

In Reset, tap the gear (Settings), then Delete my data. That removes everything on your phone, turns off reminders and deletes any shared usage.

Not medical advice

Reset is a wellness experiment, not therapy or medical care. If you're in crisis in the US, call or text 988.
