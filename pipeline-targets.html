GTM.PIPE_TARGETS

One row per time period and aggregation level for the full fiscal year. Pick a monthly, quarterly, or annual grain. Managers, regions, theaters, and the company roll up from rep-level targets.

Target Rules

Change any input and the output view recalculates. In Snowflake these live in a small config table so RevOps can update them without editing SQL.

Coverage

Pipeline needed per $1 of quota. Default is 4x, so there is enough pipeline to cover quota.

Product Mix

Share of pipeline by product. Must total 100%.

Source Mix

Where pipeline should come from. Must total 100%.

Monthly Phasing

Each month gets its share of the quarter by number of days.
