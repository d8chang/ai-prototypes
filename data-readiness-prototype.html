3. Flag sensitive data

A scan suggests columns that hold personal data, spend, or contract terms. Confirm each flag. Sensitive columns are masked or hidden by role in the next step.

PIIPersonal data

Lead emails, phone numbers, and IP addresses. Masked outside Marketing Ops and leadership.

FinancialSpend and deal values

Ad spend, campaign budgets, and opportunity amounts. Masked for roles that don't own them.

ConfidentialContracts

Vendor and agency contract terms. Hidden from the AI layer by default.
