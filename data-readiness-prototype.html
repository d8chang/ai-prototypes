3. Flag sensitive data

A scan suggests columns that hold personal, financial, or HR data. Confirm each flag. Sensitive columns are masked or hidden by role in the next step.

PIIPersonal data

Names, emails, phone numbers. Masked for everyone outside the owning team.

FinancialDeal and billing data

Contract values and payment details. Visible to Sales, Finance, and leadership.

ConfidentialHR and internal

Compensation and performance data. Hidden from the AI layer by default.
